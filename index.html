Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our transparent pricing, technicians, and parts guarantees.

How much does routine AC servicing cost?

expand_more

Our standard Jet Pump deep cleaning starts at ₹499 for split ACs. It includes high-pressure hydro washing of cooling coils, blower fan cleansing, and drainage tray clearing. We follow a strict computerized rate card with no hidden fees.

How often should I get my AC serviced in high-dust areas?

expand_more

For urban locations with construction dust or roadside pollution, we recommend deep jet cleaning every 3 to 4 months. Clogged coils increase electricity bills by up to 35% and strain the inverter compressor.

Do you provide genuine OEM spare parts with invoice and warranty?

expand_more

Yes, 100%. All capacitors, fan motors, PCB boards, and compressors supplied by COOLCARE are factory-sealed original parts backed by a official GST invoice and a 6-month instant replacement guarantee.

What does the 30-day service warranty cover?

expand_more

If your AC encounters the exact same cooling fault or water leakage within 30 days of our repair, a technician will re-visit your premises free of charge with zero diagnosis or inspection fee.

How quickly can a technician reach my doorstep in an emergency?

expand_more

Under our Express Dispatch option, a nearby mobile van technician arrives at your door within 60 to 90 minutes. You will receive real-time SMS updates and technician contact info instantly upon booking.

Which AC brands do you provide repairs and parts for?

expand_more

We service and carry OEM parts for all major HVAC brands: Daikin, Voltas, LG, Samsung, Blue Star, Panasonic, Carrier, Hitachi, O General, Mitsubishi, Godrej, and Lloyd across split and window models.
